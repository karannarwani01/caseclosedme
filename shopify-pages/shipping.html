Everything you need to know about how caseclosed gets your collectibles to you. Orders are fulfilled by Pumpy Trading L.L.C.

Processing

  • Orders are shipped via a third-party courier, Monday to Friday only.
  • Delivery time is counted from order confirmation, based on product availability.
  • The courier will attempt to contact you before delivery. Failed delivery attempts result in the parcel being returned to Pumpy Trading L.L.C for rescheduling.
  • In line with U.A.E. law, products are not offered to OFAC-sanctioned countries.
  • Multiple shipments may result in multiple postings to the cardholder's monthly statement.

Shipping Fees

Local Orders - Standard Delivery

  • A flat rate of AED 20 applies to every order, anywhere in the UAE.
  • Custom rates are quoted for remote areas outside courier coverage.

Local Orders - Cash on Delivery

  • Cash on Delivery is available across the UAE for an AED 10 handling fee, so the delivery charge is AED 30 instead of AED 20.
  • Choose the Cash on Delivery (+AED 10) delivery option at checkout to pay in cash when your order arrives. The fee is shown in your order total before you confirm.

Local Orders - Express Delivery

  • AED 35 for express delivery, available on orders placed before 11:00 AM.
  • Unavailable outside standard courier service areas.

International Orders

  • Rates are calculated after checkout; our customer service team will provide a quote.
  • Rates vary by country and location.
  • Customs fees and sales taxes are not included in shipping fees.

Damaged Items During Shipping

If an item is damaged during shipping, contact us and we will help resolve it, subject to availability and proper evaluation.

Shipping Duration

Express Delivery (UAE)

Order before the 11:00 AM cut-off for delivery within 1 to 8 business hours.

Standard Delivery (UAE)

2 to 4 business days.

International / GCC

10 to 15 business days.

Customs Clearance

International customers are responsible for all customs duties and taxes charged by the destination country.
