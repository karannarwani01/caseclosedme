Delivery & Shipping

Where do you ship to?
We ship your most-wanted collectibles anywhere in the world.

How long will my order take?
Within the UAE, orders can arrive same-day via express, or in 2-4 business days via standard delivery. International and GCC orders typically take 10-15 business days, with tracking provided. Full details are in our Shipping Policy.

Returns, Refunds & Exchanges

Can I return or exchange products?
Yes - you may return or exchange eligible merchandise, provided items stay sealed and unused. Some collectibles (and all blind boxes) are non-returnable. See our Returns / Refunds / Exchange Policy for the exact conditions and windows.

How do I request a refund?
Refunds apply to defective, damaged, or faulty items, with photos and proof of purchase. Start a request with our Refund Request Form.

Order Cancellation

Can I cancel my order?
You may cancel your order within 24 hours of placing it, subject to our Cancellation Policy. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method.

Pre-Orders

How do pre-orders work?
A deposit secures your reserved item and your slot for highly sought-after collectibles. Estimated arrival dates are a guide and may shift slightly; items ship as soon as they arrive at our warehouse. See our Pre-Order Policy.
